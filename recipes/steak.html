How to make steak

Description

Today you will learn how to make a steak. This steak will be cooked using a pan. It will use several ingriedents. At the end of this you should know how to make a basic steak. Hope you enjoy!

Ingredients

  • Rosemary
  • Thyme
  • Any steak meat of your choice
  • Butter
  • Salt and pepper
  • oil

Making the steak

Preperations

  1. Start by washing the meat.

  2. Then sprinkle and batter you salt and pepper.

  3. Let sit for 20 minutes, while waiting prepare pan with oil, have the oil ready by hearing it sizzle.

  4. Continue on to Cooking the steak.

Cooking the steak

  1. Add you steak to the pan with butter included.

  2. Give each side a minute, then add in the Rosemary and Thyme.

  3. Give each side 10 minutes each.

  4. Once done give all sides a two minutes each or one minute after it looks done.

  5. Plate the steak with the Rosemary and Thyme on the side, add in oil for the extra juice

  6. Wait five minutes or an acceptable heat for your mouth and enjoy!
