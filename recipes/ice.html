How to make Ice

Description

Today you will learn to make Ice. I don't know why your trying to learn ice. Making ice is easy but oh well. Recipe is easy enough to understand. Should take only about a day because of overnight freezing.

Ingredients and items needed

  • Water
  • Freezer
  • Tray or cup

Making the ice

  1. Start by obtaining water and putting it in either the ice cube tray of cup, if using a cup, place only an inch height of water.

  2. Place the tray/cup into a freezer.

  3. Wait overnight.

  4. If water is on top still put leave it for an extra 5 hours til you can't see or hear any water.

  5. Congrats! You made ice, don't know why you needed this to make ice but you learned to make ice!
