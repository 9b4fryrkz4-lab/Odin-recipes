How to make a Quesadilla

Description

Today you will learn how to make a simple cheese quesdilla. It's very easy trust me so it shouldn't take long. You may add more into you quesdilla such as chicken, peppers, or other ingriedents to make it personalized to you. Hope you enjoy!

Ingredients

  • Cheese
  • Torrtila
  • Other wanted vegetables, jalapenos, etc.

Making the Quesdilla

  1. Start by turning on the heat to high and placing your tortilla on the oven

  2. Keep the tortilla on the oven till its hard bendy body turns into a soft flexible one, you could also wait till orange spots start appearing, similar to the lines on the images up above.

  3. Next add in the cheese, either add it all to make space for your other toppings or add it all over to cover them all in cheese.

  4. Once the cheese has turned goey and melted either close up the tortilla and flip it over, or add in your ingriedents and then close and flip.

  5. Once done, give one, 1 minute timer to each side of the tortilla, the give one, 30 second to each side of the tortilla, then plate.

  6. Congrats! you made a Quesdilla!
